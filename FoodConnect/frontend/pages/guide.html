FoodConnect

Guide and food safety

A short checklist so every meal we move is safe to eat.

For donors

Create an account, choose Donor, and post food in the dashboard. Include the food type, servings, address, and the time by which it must be collected. Approve the organisation that can reach you soonest.

For organisations

Register as a Recipient Organisation, browse listings, and request what you can serve. Send a vehicle and containers at the agreed pickup time.

Food safety

Donate only freshly cooked or properly sealed food. Keep it covered, label the cooking time, and never post food that has already been served from or has spoiled.

Does it cost anything?

No. FoodConnect is a non-profit and free for donors and organisations.

How long does food stay safe?

Cooked food should be collected within 2 to 4 hours. Use the “Collect before” field so organisations know the limit.

Can I donate from a small home function?

Yes. Even 10 servings make a difference to a small shelter.

How do I earn a badge?

Each completed donation adds to your total servings. See the tiers on the Hall of Fame page.
